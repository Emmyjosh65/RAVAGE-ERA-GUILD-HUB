RAVAGE ERA

THE GUILD

“Built by warriors. United by RAVAGE.”

WHO WE ARE

RAVAGE ERA is a competitive Free Fire guild built around a single idea: that a group of dedicated players, bound by trust and respect, can achieve more together than any individual ever could alone. We are not just a team that queues into ranked matches together — we are a community that trains together, grows together and represents each other in every lobby we enter.

TEAMWORK & LOYALTY

At the heart of RAVAGE ERA is teamwork. Every member understands that victory in Free Fire is never earned by one player alone — it is built on callouts, coordination, covering each other's flanks and trusting the plan. Loyalty means showing up: for scrims, for squad sessions, for your teammates when a fight goes wrong. Our members are expected to be reliable, respectful and committed to the guild they represent.

COMPETITION & SKILL

RAVAGE ERA is built around competitive Free Fire. We practice squad coordination, study rotations, drops and end-game zone play, and we constantly push each other to improve. Skill is respected in RAVAGE ERA — but skill without discipline means nothing. We value players who are hungry to learn as much as players who already perform.

FRIENDSHIP & RESPECT

Beyond the rankings and the fights, RAVAGE ERA is a place where friendships are formed. We believe the best squads are the ones where players genuinely enjoy playing together. Respect is non-negotiable: respect for the guild, respect for your teammates and respect for opponents. Toxic behavior has no place here.

DISCIPLINE & GROWTH

Discipline is what separates a casual group from a real guild. Members are expected to follow the guild's direction, attend guild activities when possible and represent the RAVAGE ERA name with pride in every match. Growth is a constant goal — we improve individually so that we can improve together, and we celebrate every member's progress.

HELPING NEWER PLAYERS

RAVAGE ERA believes the guild grows stronger when everyone gets a chance to develop. Experienced members actively help newer players learn the game — sharing strategies, coaching rotations, reviewing mistakes and creating an environment where asking questions is always welcome. Nobody is left behind.

GUILD ACTIVITIES

The guild stays active through squad sessions, practice matches, tryout events and community activities. These events keep the community connected and give every member a reason to be part of the team — not just as a name on a roster, but as an active contributor to the RAVAGE ERA family.

OUR COMMUNITY

RAVAGE ERA is more than a roster — it is a community of players who share the same passion for the game and the same standards of conduct. From the Guild Leader to the newest recruit, everyone plays a part in keeping the guild strong, welcoming and competitive.

REPRESENTING RAVAGE ERA

When you wear the RAVAGE ERA name, you carry it with you into every lobby. We expect our members to play with pride, act with honor and always remember what the guild stands for: teamwork, loyalty, skill, respect and consistency. This is what we are building — and we want warriors who are ready to build it with us.
